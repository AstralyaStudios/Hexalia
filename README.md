### **About**

Hexalia is a folk-witchcraft and nature magic mod centered on herbalism, rituals, enchanted flora, and living magic.

Gather herbs and crops, refine them with the Mortar & Pestle, brew mystical mixtures, burn herbal combinations in the Censer, awaken enchanted plants, and perform Nature's Rituals using offerings, salt, and elemental forces.

Discover magical tools such as the Kelpweave Blade, Rootshaper, and Cinderhew, along with idols, protective equipment, supernatural creatures, and other forms of magic designed to blend naturally into survival and exploration.

Progression is driven by discovery and understanding rather than a skill tree, allowing Hexalia's systems to gradually become part of your home, garden, and ritual practice.

📖 **Patchouli**  
Highly recommended for Hexalia's in-game documentation through the **Verdant Grimoire**, which covers the mod's major systems, ingredients, rituals, brewing, equipment, creatures, and more.

🔎 **Recipe Viewers**  
Hexalia supports major recipe-viewing mods such as **EMI**, **JEI**, and **REI** where available.

For the best experience, **EMI is especially recommended**, offering a clean way to browse Hexalia's custom recipes and processing systems alongside standard crafting recipes.

💍 **Accessory Mods**  
Hexalia supports accessory slots through the appropriate loader integration:

*   **Accessories** on 1.21.1
*   **Trinkets** on Fabric 1.20.1
*   **Curios** on Forge 1.20.1

These integrations allow items such as rings, pendants, charms, and Earplugs to be equipped without occupying normal armor slots.

### **For Developers**

Hexalia is designed to be expandable through addons, datapacks, integrations, and modpack-specific content.

It makes extensive use of:

*   tags for expandable item and block behavior
*   data-driven recipes
*   custom recipe types
*   standard loader inventories and capabilities where applicable

You are welcome to build addons or integrations around Hexalia's existing systems and magical themes.
